A make.fun bounty entry: one line of horror, under 200 characters.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) scores six candidate
horror lines against the judge's five criteria — horror, fright,
vividness, originality, craft, weighted 5/4/3/2/1 in the brief's order —
and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

The winning line (78 characters):

> I found a photo of myself asleep, taken from inside my closet, dated tomorrow.
